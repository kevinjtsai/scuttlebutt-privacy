Privacy Policy

Scuttlebutt (Scuttlebutt Finance) · Effective October 11, 2026

Scuttlebutt is a private, invite-only app where small groups of friends share stock picks and track how they perform. This policy explains what the app collects, how it's used, and your choices. In short: we collect only what the app needs to work, we don't sell your data, and we don't show ads or track you across other apps.

What we collect

  • Account: when you sign in with Apple, we receive an account identifier and, if you choose to share them, your name and email (which may be an Apple private relay address). You set a display name and a unique @handle (so friends can tag you) that other members of your groups see.
  • What you post: your groups (name and icon), the picks you post (ticker, long or short, and your note), updates, reactions, comments, and group chat messages. When a chat message mentions a ticker (like $NVDA), we record that stock's price at that moment so the group can see how it has moved since.
  • Portfolio (optional): if you add holdings, we store the tickers and, privately, the quantities or dollar values you enter (and cost basis, if you provide it). Other members only ever see percentages: each holding's share of your portfolio and its returns. Quantities and dollar amounts are visible only to you. We also keep a history of your portfolio's percentages over time, so performance only counts holdings while you actually held them, and the name of the brokerage an account is from (for example, "E*TRADE"), never account numbers. You choose who sees your portfolio: all your groups, only groups you pick, or only you.
  • Watch list (optional): the stocks you add to your watch list, when you added each, and the stock's and the S&P 500's price at that moment (from our market data provider, never entered by you), so the app can show how it has moved since. You choose who sees your watch list separately from your portfolio: all your groups, only groups you pick, or only you. Watching a stock also turns on its big-move and upcoming-earnings notifications for you.
  • App activity: which picks you've read and when you last opened each group, so the app can show what's new, the alerts it creates for you, and your notification settings (such as a muted group chat).
  • Notifications (optional): if you allow notifications, we store your device's push token so we can send alerts about your groups. Notifications may include a short preview, such as the first words of a comment or chat message.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos library, advertising identifier, or browsing activity. Photos you pick are read only when you choose them.

Screenshots and AI

If you attach screenshots (for example, of your brokerage holdings or a stock quote), they are sent to our server and to Anthropic's AI service only to read the tickers, holdings, and brokerage name from them. Screenshots are not saved on our servers. Nothing from a screenshot is stored on our servers unless you review and confirm it. If you leave an import before saving it, the screenshots and what was read from them stay on your phone (encrypted while your phone is locked) so you can finish later; they are deleted when you save the account, tap Discard, or after 14 days. Your phone also checks the date a screenshot was taken, on the device, to warn you if it may be out of date.

When you type a pick, the text is sent to Anthropic's AI service to identify which stock it's about and whether it's bullish or bearish. AI is never used to set prices; prices always come from our market data provider.

Who can see your information

  • Members of your groups see your display name, @handle, and what you share with those groups: your picks, updates, reactions, comments, chat messages, and track record, plus your portfolio percentages if your portfolio setting allows it, and your watch list if your watch list setting allows it. A member can forward a chat message to another group they're in; it's posted there as theirs, labeled "Forwarded". People outside your groups see none of it. Because handles are unique, someone choosing a handle can find out whether a handle is already taken, but not who has it.
  • Service providers that run the app on our behalf:
    • Supabase: hosts the database, sign-in, and server functions.
    • Finnhub: provides stock prices, earnings dates, analyst ratings, and news. It receives ticker symbols only, never personal information.
    • Anthropic: AI features. It may receive news headlines, the text of picks you type or the note on a pick (to read the stock and compare it with the day's news), and screenshots you choose to attach. It does not receive your name or account details.
    • Apple: Sign in with Apple, push notifications, and app distribution (TestFlight and the App Store).

We do not sell or rent your information, and we don't share it with advertisers or data brokers. We may disclose information if required by law.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is active. Price history and market data are kept to calculate performance.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account anytime in the app (your profile → Delete account). When you do, we delete your portfolio and its history, your watch list, alerts, read history, notification tokens, @handle, and group memberships, and your sign-in can no longer be used. To keep group history accurate, picks, updates, comments, and chat messages you posted stay in your groups but are shown as from "Former member" instead of your name. You can also delete your own chat messages and comments at any time.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit, and access is restricted by database rules so people can only read what belongs to their groups. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

Not financial advice

Scuttlebutt is for sharing ideas among friends. Nothing in the app is financial, investment, or tax advice, or a recommendation to buy or sell any security.

Children

Scuttlebutt is not intended for anyone under 18, and we don't knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and, for significant changes, let you know in the app.
