Privacy Policy

Scuttlebutt (Scuttlebutt Finance) · Effective October 7, 2026

Scuttlebutt is a private, invite-only app where small groups of friends share stock picks and track how they perform. This policy explains what the app collects, how it's used, and your choices. In short: we collect only what the app needs to work, we don't sell your data, and we don't show ads or track you across other apps.

What we collect

  • Account: when you sign in with Apple, we receive an account identifier and, if you choose to share them, your name and email (which may be an Apple private relay address). You set a display name that other members of your groups see.
  • What you post: your groups, the picks you post (ticker, long or short, and your note), updates, reactions, and comments.
  • Portfolio (optional): if you add holdings, we store the tickers and, privately, the quantities or dollar values you enter (and cost basis, if you provide it). Other members only ever see percentages: each holding's share of your portfolio and its returns. Quantities and dollar amounts are visible only to you.
  • App activity: which picks you've read and when you last opened each group, so the app can show what's new, plus the alerts it creates for you.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos library, advertising identifier, or browsing activity.

Screenshots

If you attach a screenshot (for example, of your brokerage holdings or a stock quote), it is sent to our server and to Anthropic's AI service only to read the tickers and holdings from it. Screenshots are not saved. Nothing from a screenshot is stored unless you review and confirm it.

Who can see your information

  • Members of your groups see your display name and what you share with those groups: your picks, updates, reactions, comments, track record, and portfolio percentages. People outside your groups see none of it.
  • Service providers that run the app on our behalf:
    • Supabase: hosts the database, sign-in, and server functions.
    • Finnhub: provides stock prices, earnings dates, analyst ratings, and news. It receives ticker symbols only, never personal information.
    • Anthropic: AI features. It may receive news headlines, the note you wrote on a pick (to compare it with the day's news), and screenshots you choose to attach. It does not receive your name or account details.
    • Apple: Sign in with Apple and app distribution (TestFlight and the App Store).

We do not sell or rent your information, and we don't share it with advertisers or data brokers. We may disclose information if required by law.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is active. Price history and market data are kept to calculate performance.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account anytime in the app (your profile → Delete account). When you do, we delete your portfolio, alerts, read history, and group memberships, and your sign-in can no longer be used. To keep group history accurate, picks, updates, and comments you posted stay in your groups but are shown as from "Former member" instead of your name.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit, and access is restricted by database rules so people can only read what belongs to their groups. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

Not financial advice

Scuttlebutt is for sharing ideas among friends. Nothing in the app is financial, investment, or tax advice, or a recommendation to buy or sell any security.

Children

Scuttlebutt is not intended for anyone under 18, and we don't knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and, for significant changes, let you know in the app.
